Australia

Overview

Australia is a country in Oceania and comprises of the mainland of the Australian continent, the island of Tasmania and numerous smaller islands. It has a land area of 7,688,287 km², making it the sixth largest country in the world and is the flatted and driest inhabited continent. Australia’s climate varies, the north has a tropical climate, the centre is mostly desert, and the south has temperate coastal zones with cool winters. Australia is home to a rich variety of unique animals that are only found there. These include marsupials like the iconic kangaroos and Koalas, reptiles like venomous snakes and saltwater crocodiles and interesting birds such as cockatoo, kookaburra and emu.

Personal Thoughts

Australia has to be one of my favourite places as it is very similar to New Zealand but there is way more to do, especially shopping… Australia also has heaps to offer and depending on what you want to get out of your holiday depends on where in the country you go. I personally love the beach, shopping and sight seeing. I’ve found that the Sunshine Coast in QLD (Queensland) is perfect for what I like. QLD has lots to offer and majority of cities and towns lay along gorgeous beaches. I have stayed two times in Maloolaba which is north of Brisbane and is close to tourist hotspots like Australia Zoo and Noosa. My highlight from my last trip was getting to watch the Irwins do the croc shows at Australia Zoo! even got to say hi to Robert Irwin himself which was such a cool experience. Even when the Irwin’s are not there it is still a must go place as it is huge and the enclosures are incredible, you hardly feel like your in a zoo most of the time. I also really love going to Noosa, however it is very popular so if you want a spot on the beach make sure you go early!
